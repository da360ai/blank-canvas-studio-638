

# DA360 — High-Converting Leadership Landing Page

## Overview
A premium, CRO-optimized landing page for DA360's digital marketing leadership program. Dark, bold aesthetic with red CTAs, outcome-driven copy, and conversion-focused structure.

## Brand System
- **Colors**: Dark hero (#0D0D12), White sections, Red CTAs (#E50914), Yellow accents (#F4C542)
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts — Syne (headings), DM Sans (body)
- **Style**: Card-based, high spacing, soft shadows, rounded corners

## Page Sections (in order)

### 1. Sticky Navbar
Logo, NSDC/Skill India badges, phone number, "Book Free Class" red CTA, floating WhatsApp button

### 2. Hero Section (Dark bg)
- Split layout: bold headline left, lead capture form right
- Persona toggle (Fresher / Working Professional)
- Trust strip: 50K+ Alumni | 1000+ Placements | 22+ Certifications
- Countdown timer + scarcity ("Only X seats left")
- Red CTA: "Book Free Leadership Masterclass"

### 3. Pain Point Section
Emotional cards — job rejections, stagnant roles, AI anxiety → "There is a better way"

### 4. 3-Pillar Value Proposition
Three outcome-focused cards: Learn Like a Leader, Work Like a Pro, Land Your Dream Role

### 5. Social Proof Strip
Animated counters: 50K+ Alumni, 1000+ Placements, ₹6.8L Avg Salary, 22+ Certs, 20+ Campaigns

### 6. Who Is This For
4 persona cards (Graduates, Professionals, Entrepreneurs, Switchers) with pain/outcome/CTA

### 7. Curriculum Section (Outcome-based)
Visual outcome cards (not module lists) + Download Curriculum CTA

### 8. Projects Section
Grid of student project cards with campaign dashboards and portfolio previews

### 9. Mentor Section
Mentor cards with photos, credentials, company logos, LinkedIn links

### 10. Testimonials (Video-first)
Video thumbnails with name, company, salary transformation, LinkedIn

### 11. Outcomes / Transformation
Before→After salary cards, job roles achieved, hiring company logo grid

### 12. Comparison Table
DA360 vs Others — checkmark comparison highlighting DA360 advantages

### 13. Risk Reversal
Free masterclass, low-risk messaging, trust guarantees

### 14. Journey / Funnel
Visual timeline: Learn → Practice → Internship → Placement

### 15. Urgency Section
Countdown timer, limited seats, batch closing alert

### 16. Final CTA
Bold headline + two buttons: Book Free Class, Apply Now

### 17. Footer
Contact, WhatsApp, social links, trust badges

## CRO Elements Throughout
- CTAs every 2-3 sections
- Countdown timers with urgency
- Scarcity messaging
- WhatsApp floating button as friction reducer
